## 2. Project Vision & Challenge Alignment
We are building a web-based agricultural decision-support dashboard that helps farmers evaluate crop suitability and compare crop-rotation strategies (baseline vs. adaptive alternatives) under shifting climate conditions.

### Core User Flow
1. **Locate & Draw Farm:** A farmer opens the web app, which automatically retrieves their device geolocation (`navigator.geolocation`) and centers an interactive **OpenStreetMap** basemap on their location. The farmer clicks points on the map to draw their farm's boundary polygon, generating a **WGS84 (`EPSG:4326`) GeoJSON** polygon and computing the farm's centroid `(lat, lon)` and area.
2. **Ingest Environmental & Satellite Data:** The backend uses the farm's centroid and GeoJSON bounding box to fetch historical/recent weather time-series and stream satellite imagery for a selected timeframe.
3. **Compute Plant & Soil Health Indicators:** Process spectral bands into vegetation indices (**NDVI**) while ingesting direct Earth observation layers (**SMAP** soil moisture, **ECOSTRESS** evapotranspiration/thermal stress).
4. **Score & Rank Crops (FAO-56):** Match the farm's environmental profile against crop parameters from **FAO-56** (water demand $K_c$, root depth $Z_r$, growing window, water-stress sensitivity $K_y$, and nitrogen contribution) to **score and rank plants from worst to best** for planting, as well as compare multi-crop rotation strategies.
5. **Simulate & Export:** Run a **$+2^\circ\text{C}$ warming simulation** comparing plant performance and ideal health against historical/current baselines, and provide a one-click **"Print / Export Dashboard as PDF"** button.

## 3. Technical Architecture & Open Data Pipeline (6-Module Roadmap)

* **Module 1 — Interactive Map & GeoJSON Intake (Frontend):**
  * OpenStreetMap tiles (Leaflet.js / React-Leaflet or MapLibre) + polygon drawing tools (`Leaflet.draw` / `Geoman`).
  * Browser geolocation auto-centering + fallback coordinate search / demo farm presets.
  * Outputs validated GeoJSON Polygon, bounding box (`bbox`), and centroid `(lat, lon)`.

* **Module 2 — Agro-Climatology Service (NASA POWER API):**
  * Query `https://power.larc.nasa.gov/api/temporal/daily/point` (`community=AG`) using the farm centroid.
  * Extract daily `T2M`, `T2M_MAX`, `T2M_MIN`, `PRECTOTCORR` (precipitation), and `ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN` (solar radiation) to calculate historical averages, Growing Degree Days ($GDD$), and Reference Evapotranspiration ($ET_0$).

* **Module 3 — Satellite Imagery & Raster Streaming (STAC + COG):**
  * Query **NASA Earthdata CMR-STAC** (`https://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/stac/LPCLOUD` or `ALL` via `pystac-client`) filtered by farm `bbox` and date window.
  * Stream **Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFFs (COGs)** via `rioxarray` / `stackstac` (HTTP range requests clipped strictly to the farm polygon):
    * **Computed Indices:** **NDVI** (and optional EVI) from Landsat 8/9 or HLS/MODIS bands to track canopy health and bare-soil windows.
    * **Direct Datasets:** **SMAP** (topsoil & root-zone soil moisture) and **ECOSTRESS** (actual evapotranspiration $ET_a$ & plant thermal stress).
    * **Optional Static Baselines:** **SRTM** (30m elevation/slope for runoff risk) and **SoilGrids 2.0 REST API** (soil texture & available water capacity).

* **Module 4 — FAO-56 Crop & Rotation Scoring Engine:**
  * Use **FAO-56** tables (`pyfao56` or structured JSON lookup derived from FAO-56 + USDA PLANTS nitrogen fixation data).
  * Calculate crop water demand ($ET_c = ET_0 \times K_c$) and relative yield/stress penalty using the FAO Water-Yield Stress equation:
    $$\left(1 - \frac{Y_a}{Y_m}\right) = K_y \times \left(1 - \frac{ET_a}{ET_c}\right)$$
  * Score each candidate crop against current moisture, heat stress, and growing window, ranking them **worst to best**, and evaluate multi-crop rotation sequences (comparing baseline monoculture vs. nitrogen-fixing legumes and deep-rooting cover crops).

* **Module 5 — $+2^\circ\text{C}$ Climate Scenario Simulator:**
  * Perturb daily temperature series by $+2^\circ\text{C}$, recompute $ET_0$, $GDD$, and thermal stress penalties, and compare **Historical/Current Farm State vs. $+2^\circ\text{C}$ Future Projection vs. Ideal Crop Health Benchmarks**.

* **Module 6 — Decision-Support Dashboard & PDF Export:**
  * Clean UI presenting farm boundary + NDVI/SMAP layers, worst-to-best crop suitability leaderboard, rotation strategy comparison cards, $+2^\circ\text{C}$ comparison charts, and a **"Print / Save as PDF"** button (`@media print` CSS or `html2pdf.js` / `jspdf`).
